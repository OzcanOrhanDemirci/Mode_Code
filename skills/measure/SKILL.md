---
name: measure
description: Use before giving numbers about speed, size, memory, counts, coverage or quota, or calling something faster or fixed: measure with a command, or label the number as an estimate.
---

A number in an answer is a measurement or it is labeled as an estimate.

1. **Measure with a command** whose output you show or quote: `time` or the tool's own timer for speed; `wc -c`, `du`, the build's report for size; the test runner's count; `mode-code quota` and `mode-code tokens` for the quota.
2. **Compare like with like.** Before and after under the same conditions (same input, same machine, a warm or a cold run on both sides, several runs when the result varies), or say what differed.
3. **Say how it was measured** in one line beside the number: the command, the input, how many runs.
4. If it cannot be measured here, say so and give a range with what it rests on, marked as an estimate. Never round a guess into a figure that reads as measured.
